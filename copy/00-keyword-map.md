## Rules the build must follow

1. **One H1 per page**, containing or adjacent to the primary keyword.
2. **Keyword density 0.5–1.5%.** The primary keyword appears in: title tag, meta description, H1 area, first 100 words, one H2, one FAQ question, and the image alt text. That is enough. Do not stuff.
3. **Title tags ≤ 60 characters. Meta descriptions 150–160 characters.** Both are written for you on each page file.
4. **FAQ sections ship with `FAQPage` schema.** This is the single highest-ROI schema for this business — it wins AI Overview and ChatGPT citations, where a lot of "ai receptionist" and "google review service" research now happens.
5. **Every page ends with the same CTA.** One offer, one button, repeated. Minimal sites convert because they remove choices, not because they remove words.
6. **Anything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` is a placeholder you must replace with a real number, name, or result.** Do not publish invented statistics or testimonials — fabricated proof is both an E-E-A-T liability and an FTC one.
