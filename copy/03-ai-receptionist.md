## SECTION 3 — What it does

**H2:** What the AI receptionist actually handles

**Answers on the first ring, 24/7/365**
No hold music, no phone tree, no "please leave a message after the tone."

**Talks like a person, not a robot**
Natural voice, natural pace, handles interruptions and follow-up questions. It's trained on your services, your service area, your hours, and your pricing rules.

**Answers real questions**
"Do you work in [city]?" "How much is a service call?" "How soon can someone come out?" "Do you take [insurance/payment type]?" It answers from what you told it — and says "let me have the owner confirm that" instead of making something up.

**Books the appointment**
Checks your live calendar availability, offers real slots, books it, and sends the customer a confirmation text.

**Texts you instantly**
Caller name, number, what they need, and how urgent it is — in your pocket within seconds.

**Escalates when it should**
Emergencies, big jobs, or anything outside its script get transferred to you or your on-call tech immediately. You set the rules.

**Follows up on missed calls**
If a call somehow slips through, the system sends an automatic text back within seconds so the conversation continues instead of ending.

**Logs everything**
Every call recorded, transcribed, and pushed into your CRM. You finally get a real count of how many people are calling and what they're calling about.

## SECTION 8 — FAQ

**H2:** Questions about AI receptionists

**What is an AI receptionist?**
An AI receptionist is a voice agent that answers your business phone, holds a real conversation with the caller, answers questions about your services, and books appointments into your calendar — 24 hours a day, without a person on the line.

**Will callers know it's AI?**
Some will, some won't. We don't pretend it's human — the agent introduces itself clearly, because callers who feel tricked don't book. What they care about is that someone picked up and solved their problem in 40 seconds.

**What happens if it can't answer a question?**
It says so honestly, takes the caller's details, and either transfers to you live or flags it for a callback. It's built not to invent answers about pricing or availability.

**Do I need to change my phone number?**
No. You keep your existing number and forward it to the agent — either all calls, or only the ones you don't answer.

**Can it book directly into my calendar?**
Yes. It connects to [Google Calendar / your scheduling tool / your CRM — list what you support] and offers only slots you're actually free for.

**How is this different from an answering service?**
A human answering service takes a message and passes it along, usually without knowing your pricing or availability. The AI receptionist knows your business, handles unlimited calls at once, works nights and weekends without a surcharge, and closes the loop by booking the job.
