## SECTION 2 — The problem

**H2:** A pretty website that doesn't convert is an expensive brochure

Most service business websites fail the same four ways:

- **They're slow.** Every extra second of load time on mobile costs you visitors — and most of your traffic is someone standing in a driveway on a phone.
- **They bury the phone number.** If a visitor has to hunt for how to contact you, they've already opened a competitor's tab.
- **They talk about the company, not the customer.** "Founded in 2009 with a passion for excellence" answers a question nobody asked.
- **They have no clear next step.** A site can be beautiful and still leave the visitor with nothing obvious to click.

Traffic isn't the problem when the page gives people nowhere to go.

**How long does it take to build?**
Two to four weeks for most service businesses, measured from the day we have your content and access. The most common delay is waiting on photos and logins — get those ready and we move fast.

**Will my new website rank on Google?**
The build gives you the technical foundation that ranking requires: speed, mobile usability, clean structure, schema, and a separate page per service.
