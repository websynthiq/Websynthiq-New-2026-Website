The problem

A pretty website that doesn't convert is an expensive brochure

Most service business websites fail the same four ways:

Failure / 01

They're slow.

Every extra second of load time on mobile costs you visitors — and most of your traffic is someone standing in a driveway on a phone.

Failure / 02

They bury the phone number.

If a visitor has to hunt for how to contact you, they've already opened a competitor's tab.

Failure / 03

They talk about the company, not the customer.

"Founded in 2009 with a passion for excellence" answers a question nobody asked.

Failure / 04

They have no clear next step.

A site can be beautiful and still leave the visitor with nothing obvious to click.

Traffic isn't the problem when the page gives people nowhere to go.

FAQ

Common questions about small business web design

How much do small business web design services cost? +

Price moves with the number of service pages, whether you need copywriting and photography, and whether you're adding booking or payment functionality. You get a fixed quote before any work begins — no line-item surprises.

How long does it take to build? +

Two to four weeks for most service businesses, measured from the day we have your content and access. The most common delay is waiting on photos and logins — get those ready and we move fast.

Will my new website rank on Google? +

The build gives you the technical foundation that ranking requires: speed, mobile usability, clean structure, schema, and a separate page per service. Rankings then come from ongoing signals — reviews, content, and links. A well-built site makes ranking possible; it doesn't make it automatic, and anyone promising you page one in 30 days is guessing.

Do I need a redesign, or just fixes? +

Sometimes just fixes. If your site is fast and structurally sound but converting poorly, a copy and layout overhaul is cheaper and quicker than a rebuild. We'll tell you which one you need on the audit call, even when the answer is the smaller invoice.

Do I own the website? +

Yes — you own the domain, the content, and the site itself. The exact terms are stated in writing before the build starts.

Can you write the content? +

Yes. Most service business owners don't have time to write a dozen service pages, so copywriting is part of the build. We interview you once and write from that.
