The diagnosis

## Most service businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have a leak.

You're already getting attention. The issue is what happens next.

Leak / 01

### A caller reaches voicemail.

Most people who hit voicemail don't leave a message. They call the next company on the list instead and book with whoever answers.

Leak / 02

### Your website looks fine but says nothing.

Visitors can't tell in five seconds what you do, where you work, or what happens if they click. So they leave.

Leak / 03

### You have 14 Google reviews. The competitor two towns over has 340.

Google reads that as an answer about who's better. So do customers.

Leak / 04

### You boosted a post once and got 40 clicks and zero jobs.

That wasn't a campaign. It was a donation.

Fix the leaks first. Then turn up the volume. Doing it in the other order is how businesses spend thousands a month to stay exactly where they are.
