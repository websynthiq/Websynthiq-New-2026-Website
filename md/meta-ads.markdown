The problem

## Boosting posts is not advertising

Most service businesses have already tried Facebook ads. Here's how it usually went:

Went wrong / 01

### You boosted a post.

It reached 4,000 people, got 60 likes, and produced zero jobs. Boosting optimizes for engagement, not customers.

Went wrong / 02

### The leads were junk.

Form fills from people 40 miles outside your area who don't remember opting in, because the ad promised nothing specific.

Went wrong / 03

### Nobody called them back fast enough.

A Meta lead goes cold in minutes. If it sits in an inbox until evening, it's gone.

Went wrong / 04

### You couldn't tell what worked.

No pixel, no conversion tracking, no offline conversion data, so Meta's algorithm was optimizing blind and so were you.

The platform isn't the problem. Running it like a social media account instead of a lead machine is.

FAQ

## Questions about Meta ads for service businesses

How much should a small business spend on Meta ads?

Start with enough daily budget for each ad set to reach about 50 conversions within 7 days. That's how Meta exits the learning phase and its optimization gets reliable. Spending less doesn't produce a smaller version of the result; it usually produces no result at all. We'll give you the specific number for your market on the audit call.

How long before Meta ads work?

Meta's learning phase ends once an ad set gets about 50 conversions within 7 days. After that, give it 30–60 days before you can judge the campaign fairly. Anyone who tells you day-three numbers mean something is reading noise.

Facebook ads or Google Ads: which is better for my business?

Google catches people at the moment they search. Meta puts you in front of homeowners in your service area before they need you, so when the AC quits or a pipe bursts, yours is the name they already recognize. It also creates demand for the jobs people put off (tune-ups, upgrades, remodels, seasonal maintenance) and usually costs less per lead. On the audit call I'll show you what that looks like for your trade.

Why were my Facebook leads such bad quality?

Usually three causes: a vague offer that attracts everyone, no qualifying step in the form, and slow follow-up. We fix all three: a specific offer, qualifying questions, and automated contact within minutes.

Do you handle the creative?

Yes. We write the copy and edit the video and images. We'll need raw footage and photos from your jobs. Real footage consistently outperforms stock, and we'll tell you exactly what to capture with your phone.

Do I need a separate landing page?

Yes, and it's not optional. Homepages are built to serve everyone; ad traffic needs one page that continues the exact promise from the ad. It's included in the build.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your Business Manager, so if we ever part ways, your account, pixel, audiences, and data stay with you.
